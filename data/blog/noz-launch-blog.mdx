Last month we wrote about [bringing agent-native observability to SigNoz](https://signoz.io/blog/introducing-agent-native-observability/). The idea was simple. The platform we have spent years building, open source, OpenTelemetry-native, with logs, metrics, and traces correlated in one database, is also a strong foundation for AI agents. The hosted MCP server was the first piece. Today we are shipping the next one.

Meet Noz, your AI teammate inside SigNoz. Ask it about your telemetry or how SigNoz works, and it answers from your own data and the docs. Tell it to set up an alert or a dashboard and it builds it for you.

Noz is in beta and free to use while it is in beta.

## Why we built Noz

We did not start with "we should add an AI chatbot." We started with what users were already doing.

SigNoz has had human chat support built into the product for a long time. Over the past year we noticed something. A lot of users assumed that chat was an AI, and they treated it like one. They asked it how to build a dashboard, why their ingestion looked off, how to write a query for a specific service. The demand for an in-product assistant was already there. They expected instant answers, the way you would from an AI, and some even apologized when they realized a person had been replying all along.

That told us two things. Users want answers inside SigNoz, at the moment they are stuck, without leaving for the docs or a separate tool. And the questions they ask map to the same mechanical steps that slow everyone down, like finding the right data, remembering attribute names, and turning an intent into an alert or a dashboard.

So we built Noz to remove that friction, not to bolt an AI chat onto the product because everyone else is doing it. Noz is a teammate, not a replacement for your engineers. It does the grunt work, finding the data, writing the query, building the artifact, while you stay in control of the decisions. When it handles the mechanical parts, you spend less time getting to signal.

## What you can do with Noz

Noz lives in a sidepane next to whatever you are looking at, so it has context on the page you are on. The clearest way to see what it does is an alert. Tell Noz "alert me when checkout p99 latency goes above 2 seconds" and it builds the alert rule.

It works the same way across the rest of SigNoz. Describe a dashboard and Noz helps assemble the panels. Ask about error rates, slow traces, or log volume for a service and it answers from your data, with a link into the explorer when you want to dig deeper.

These are a few examples. Start with whatever you are trying to get done in SigNoz, and you might be surprised by how much it already handles.

## Noz, or the MCP server with Claude and Codex?

This is a fair question, especially since we shipped the [hosted MCP server](https://signoz.io/docs/ai/signoz-mcp-server/) last month. Both connect AI to your SigNoz data. They are built for different moments, and we use both ourselves.

Reach for Noz when you are working inside SigNoz and want zero setup. It is already there in the sidepane, authenticated with your session, aware of the page you are on, and able to deep-link results back into the explorer. That makes Noz a good fit for anything that runs through the SigNoz UI, which an agent working from your terminal cannot drive.

Reach for the MCP server with Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex when your investigation reaches past SigNoz. If you need to correlate a spike with a recent commit, a GitHub issue, or a Slack thread, an external agent with the MCP server can pull SigNoz data alongside your other tools in one place.

Same open foundation, two entry points. Noz is the fastest path inside the product. The MCP server is the bridge to everything outside it.

## What's next

Noz today is focused on the work inside SigNoz. Two directions we are building toward.

**Deeper integrations with other tools.** The investigations that matter rarely stay inside one tool. As Noz connects to the systems your team already uses, it can pull that context into an investigation instead of leaving you to stitch it together by hand. The more it can reach, the more capable it gets.

**More advanced SRE workflows.** Noz already investigates, correlating signals and narrowing down likely causes. We are making it smarter at that and useful for more of the situations your team runs into, so over time it takes on more of the repetitive incident work and helps bring down MTTR. Proactiveness is coming too, so Noz can start an investigation on its own when an alert fires instead of waiting for you to ask.

Noz stays a teammate through all of this. It does more of the work, you keep making the calls. That is what [agent-native observability](https://signoz.io/blog/introducing-agent-native-observability/) looks like in the product.

## Get started

Noz is in beta and live in your SigNoz Cloud workspace, in the sidepane and on a full-screen page of its own. There is nothing to install. Open it and ask your first question.

It is free while it is in beta. When it leaves beta, pricing will be transparent and easy to reason about, the same way we approach the rest of SigNoz pricing.

A good first thing to try is to ask Noz to create an alert for a service you care about. And if a teammate would benefit, invite them in so they can use it too.
