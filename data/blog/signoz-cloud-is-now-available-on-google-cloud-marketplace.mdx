We’re excited to bring SigNoz Cloud to [Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/signoz-marketplace/signoz-cloud). Google Cloud customers can now subscribe to our OpenTelemetry-native observability platform through Marketplace, with billing managed through Google Cloud and the service operated and supported by SigNoz.

SigNoz already helps teams monitor applications and infrastructure running on Google Cloud. Through OpenTelemetry instrumentation and our Google Cloud integrations, teams can bring application telemetry and cloud service data into one place. Marketplace availability builds on this existing support, giving teams a way to purchase SigNoz Cloud within the Google Cloud purchasing process they already use.

## Observability for your applications on Google Cloud

SigNoz brings logs, metrics, and traces together in one OpenTelemetry-native platform.

When an application slows down, you can follow a request across services, examine its related logs, and look at application and infrastructure metrics as part of the same investigation.

Teams running on Google Cloud can also collect metrics and logs from supported services, including Compute Engine, Google Kubernetes Engine, and Cloud SQL, through the [SigNoz GCP integration](https://signoz.io/docs/integrations/gcp/gcp-integration/). Application traces are collected through OpenTelemetry instrumentation.

With SigNoz Cloud, the SigNoz team manages the observability backend, including storage, upgrades, and scaling.

## Get started

Visit [SigNoz Cloud on Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/signoz-marketplace/signoz-cloud) to review the subscription plan and pricing.

Once your account is ready, follow the [SigNoz Cloud setup guides](https://signoz.io/docs/cloud/) to connect your applications and send telemetry.
