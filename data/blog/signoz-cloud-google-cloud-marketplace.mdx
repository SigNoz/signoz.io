SigNoz Cloud, our OpenTelemetry-native observability platform, is now available on [Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/signoz-marketplace/signoz-cloud). You can subscribe through Marketplace and manage your SigNoz billing through Google Cloud, while SigNoz runs and supports the service.

## Bring your telemetry together with SigNoz Cloud

SigNoz Cloud gives engineering teams one place to monitor applications and infrastructure, investigate production issues, and configure alerts.

- **Application monitoring and tracing:** Track latency, errors, and throughput, and follow requests across services to identify bottlenecks.
- **Log management:** Search application logs and connect them with traces for more context during investigations.
- **Infrastructure monitoring:** Monitor hosts and Kubernetes resources alongside application telemetry.
- **Alerting:** Configure alerts across metrics, logs, and traces.

Your applications use OpenTelemetry for instrumentation. SigNoz manages the backend, including storage, upgrades, and scaling.

## Purchase and billing through Google Cloud

The Marketplace listing gives Google Cloud customers a way to purchase SigNoz Cloud within their cloud procurement process.

- **Subscribe through Marketplace:** Review the subscription plan and usage pricing directly on the listing.
- **Billing handled by Google:** Manage your SigNoz Marketplace subscription through Google Cloud.
- **Product support from SigNoz:** Contact the SigNoz team for help with the platform.

## Get started

Explore [SigNoz Cloud on Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/product/signoz-marketplace/signoz-cloud) to review the plan and subscribe.

Once your account is ready, follow the [SigNoz Cloud documentation](https://signoz.io/docs/cloud/) to connect your applications and start sending telemetry.
