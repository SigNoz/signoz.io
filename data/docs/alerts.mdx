Alerts in SigNoz give you proactive monitoring across all telemetry signals. Configure alert rules on metrics, logs, traces, anomalies, exceptions, or Apdex scores, and route notifications to the right team through your preferred channels.

## Key capabilities

- **6 alert types**: Cover every signal and use case — from threshold-based metric alerts to anomaly detection based on historical baselines.
- **Routing policies**: Direct alert notifications to the right team or channel based on labels, severity, or service ownership.
- **Planned maintenance windows**: Suppress alerts during scheduled downtime to avoid alert fatigue from known events.
- **Alert history**: Review a full history of alert firings and resolutions to understand patterns and recurrence.
- **Terraform support**: Manage alert rules as code using the [SigNoz Terraform provider](/docs/alerts-management/terraform-provider-signoz/) for repeatable, version-controlled alert configuration.

## Alert types

- [Metrics](/docs/alerts-management/metrics-based-alerts/) — Trigger alerts when a metric query crosses a threshold.
- [Logs](/docs/alerts-management/log-based-alerts/) — Fire alerts on log patterns, counts, or attribute conditions.
- [Traces](/docs/alerts-management/trace-based-alerts/) — Alert on span conditions like high p99 latency or elevated error counts.
- [Anomaly](/docs/alerts-management/anomaly-based-alerts/) — Alert when a metric deviates from its expected historical baseline.
- [Exceptions](/docs/alerts-management/exceptions-based-alerts/) — Alert on new or recurring exceptions from instrumented services.
- [Apdex](/docs/alerts-management/apdex-alerts/) — Alert when user satisfaction scores fall below a target.
