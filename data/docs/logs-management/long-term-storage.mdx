SigNoz Cloud supports two storage tiers for logs: **Hot storage** for fast queries and **Cold storage** for long-term retention at lower cost. You configure how long data stays in each tier.

## Performance characteristics

| | Hot storage | Cold storage |
|---|---|---|
| Query speed | Fast | ~3× slower than hot |
| Use case | Active debugging, recent incidents | Compliance, audit, historical analysis |

Query performance in cold storage depends on the specific query — the 3× estimate applies to typical workloads.

## Enable this feature

Long-term storage is available for SigNoz Cloud accounts. Contact support via the in-product chat (bottom-right corner of your SigNoz instance) or email cloud-support@signoz.io to enable it and configure your retention tiers.
