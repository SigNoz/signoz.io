SigNoz provides various options for sending logs, allowing you to choose the best method based on your infrastructure, control requirements, and desired telemetry richness.

Overall, there are two primary approaches to log collection: **[Application-level](#application-level-collection-otlp)** and **[Infrastructure-level](#infrastructure-level-collection-agent)**.

## Application-level Collection (OTLP)

In this method, your application uses an OpenTelemetry SDK or Agent to send logs directly to SigNoz.

### How it works

The OpenTelemetry SDK runs inside your application process. It intercepts logs from your logging library (like Log4j2, Logback, Winston, Pino) and exports them via OTLP.

### Why choose this?

- **Trace-Log Correlation**: Automatic injection of `trace_id` and `span_id` which allows you to jump from a trace to the exact logs generated during that request.
- **Rich Context**: Automatically captures application-level metadata (functions, classes, thread IDs).
- **No Sidecar/DeamonSet**: Doesn't require separate infrastructure agents, the app is self-contained.

### When to use

- You have control over the application code/startup.
- You need deep observability and correlation with traces.

## Infrastructure-level Collection (Agent)

In this method, your application writes logs to `stdout`/`stderr` or a file, and a separate agent (running on the host, node, or sidecar) collects, parses, and sends them to SigNoz.

### How it works

Your app knows nothing about SigNoz. It just logs to console or disk. An OpenTelemetry Collector (or FluentBit/Vector) reads these streams, parses them, and forwards them.

### Why choose this?

- **Non-intrusive**: No code changes or dependencies required in the application.
- **Centralized Management**: Configure log collection rules (filtering, masking) in one place for all apps.
- **Polyglot Support**: Works for any process that writes logs, including legacy apps, databases, and third-party software.

### When to use

- You are running on Kubernetes or Docker.
- You cannot modify the application (legacy/vendor apps).
