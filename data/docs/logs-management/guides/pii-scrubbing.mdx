## Overview

PII scrubbing replaces sensitive data patterns — email addresses, social security numbers, credit card numbers — with placeholders before logs are stored. This protects user privacy and helps you comply with GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations without modifying your application code.

:::info Prerequisites
- [OpenTelemetry Collector](https://signoz.io/docs/opentelemetry-collection-agents/get-started/) installed and running
- Application logs flowing into the collector (via OTLP, filelog receiver, or any other OTel receiver)
:::

## Steps

### Step 1: Identify PII patterns in your logs

Inspect a sample log to find what sensitive data needs masking. For example:

```json
{
  "timestamp": "2024-07-05T12:34:56Z",
  "severity": "INFO",
  "message": "User john.doe@example.com (SSN: 123-45-6789) made a purchase with credit card 4111-1111-1111-1111",
  "user_id": "12345",
  "purchase_amount": 99.99
}
```

This log contains three PII types: an email address, a Social Security Number, and a credit card number.

### Step 2: Configure the transform processor

Add the transform processor to the `processors` section of your `config.yaml`. Use `replace_pattern` with a regex for each PII type:

```yaml
processors:
  transform/scrub_pii:
    log_statements:
      - context: log
        statements:
          # Mask the username portion of email addresses
          - replace_pattern(body, "(\\w+)@(\\w+)\\.(\\w+)", "****@$2.$3")
          # Mask Social Security Numbers (###-##-####)
          - replace_pattern(body, "\\d{3}-\\d{2}-\\d{4}", "***-**-****")
          # Mask credit card numbers (####-####-####-####)
          - replace_pattern(body, "\\d{4}-\\d{4}-\\d{4}-\\d{4}", "****-****-****-****")
```

Adjust the regular expressions to match the exact PII formats in your logs. Add more `replace_pattern` statements for additional data types.

### Step 3: Add the processor to your pipeline

```yaml
service:
  pipelines:
    logs:
      receivers: [otlp, filelog/app]
      processors: [transform/scrub_pii, batch]
      exporters: [otlp]
```

### Step 4: Restart the collector

Apply your changes by restarting the OpenTelemetry Collector.

## Verify

After restarting, generate a log with PII and check **Logs → Logs Explorer** in SigNoz. The sample from Step 1 should appear scrubbed:

```json
{
  "message": "User ****@example.com (SSN: ***-**-****) made a purchase with credit card ****-****-****-****",
  "user_id": "12345",
  "purchase_amount": 99.99
}
```
