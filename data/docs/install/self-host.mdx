title: Install Self-Hosted SigNoz
description: Choose a Self-Hosted SigNoz installation for Docker, Linux, or Kubernetes when your team will operate the observability platform.

---

Self-Hosted SigNoz runs in your environment. Your team operates its storage, scaling, upgrades, security, and availability. If you want SigNoz to manage the platform, use [SigNoz Cloud](https://signoz.io/docs/cloud/).
