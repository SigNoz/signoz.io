SigNoz supports ingesting metrics from a wide range of sources, including OpenTelemetry SDKs, the OpenTelemetry Collector, and Prometheus.

## Ways to send metrics

- **OpenTelemetry SDKs**: Instrument your application code to capture custom metrics.
- **OpenTelemetry Collector**: Collect infrastructure metrics (host, K8s, Docker) and receive metrics from other agents. [Learn more](/docs/userguide/otel-collector-metrics).
- **Prometheus**: Send existing Prometheus metrics to SigNoz via remote write or by scraping exporters. [Learn more](/docs/userguide/prometheus-metrics).
