### Q. How to view metrics?

You can explore all ingested metrics in the [Metrics Explorer](/docs/metrics-management/metrics-explorer/).
Or you can also [Add a panel](https://signoz.io/docs/userguide/manage-dashboards/#add-a-panel-to-a-dashboard) in a Dashboard to view the metrics in dropdown.

### Why I can't see my metric 'x'?

Common causes include incorrect query filters or a time range that doesn't overlap with ingestion. Verify the metric is appearing in [Metrics Explorer](/docs/metrics-management/metrics-explorer/) for your selected time range.

**Temporality and mixed temporalities:**

SigNoz handles metrics with both cumulative and delta temporality simultaneously. Switching a metric's temporality (for example, from cumulative to delta) does not break queries over previously stored data.

For more information about metric temporality, see [Metric Types and Aggregation](https://signoz.io/docs/metrics-management/types-and-aggregation/#temporality-of-metrics).
