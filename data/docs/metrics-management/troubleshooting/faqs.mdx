### Q. How to view metrics?

You can explore all ingested metrics in the [Metrics Explorer](https://signoz.io/docs/metrics-management/metrics-explorer/).
Or you can also [Add a panel](https://signoz.io/docs/userguide/manage-dashboards/#add-a-panel-to-a-dashboard) in a Dashboard to view the metrics in dropdown.

### Why I can't see my metric 'x'?

You see this when the query filter is wrong, or when the time range holds no ingested data. Make sure that the metric is present in [Metrics Explorer](https://signoz.io/docs/metrics-management/metrics-explorer/) for the selected time range.

**Mixed temporality:**

SigNoz queries Sum metrics that carry both cumulative and delta data points. If you change the temporality of a Sum metric from cumulative to delta, your queries over the older cumulative data still work.

For more information about metric temporality, see [Metric Types and Aggregation](https://signoz.io/docs/metrics-management/types-and-aggregation/#temporality-of-metrics).
