## DORA Metrics

The [CI/CD Dashboard](https://signoz.io/docs/dashboards/dashboard-templates/cicd/) tracks two DORA metrics directly: change lead time and deployment frequency. Read [CI/CD Observability Powered by OpenTelemetry](https://signoz.io/blog/ci-cd-observability-opentelemetry/) to build DORA metrics from OpenTelemetry's CI/CD semantic conventions.
