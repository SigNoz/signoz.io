The **Trace Details** page renders the full journey of a request through your system. It handles traces with over a million spans and provides synchronized flamegraph and waterfall views for analysis.

<YouTube id="oQdjfhzz5oQ" mute={false} />

## Opening a trace

Select any trace from the Trace Explorer to open the Trace Details page. You will see two synchronized views:

- **Flame Graph** — hierarchical view showing nested span relationships.
- **Waterfall Chart** — timeline view showing the sequence and duration of each span.

## Inspecting a span

Click any span in the flamegraph or waterfall to inspect it:

- **Span context and attributes** — key-value metadata attached to the span.
- **Span events** — timestamped log messages recorded during the span.
- **Span links** — references to related spans across async operations.
- **Span kind** — client, server, producer, consumer, or internal.

**Span Percentile** — when you open a span, SigNoz shows its latency percentile (p50, p90, p95, p99) relative to other spans from the same service over the past hour. Click the percentile to see the full distribution.

## Search and filter

Use the search bar in the Trace Details view to filter spans by attribute, error status, or span name. Enable `has_error = true` to highlight all error spans.
