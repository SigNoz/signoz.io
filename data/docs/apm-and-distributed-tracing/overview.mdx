APM & Distributed Tracing in SigNoz gives you end-to-end visibility into your application's health and request flow. Monitor service-level RED metrics (Rate, Errors, Duration) and trace individual requests as they travel across microservices — all in one place.

## Key capabilities

- **Services view**: Monitor every service with [latency percentiles, error rates, requests per second, and Apdex scores](/docs/userguide/metrics/) at a glance — plus [database call](/docs/apm-and-distributed-tracing/database-calls/) and [external call](/docs/apm-and-distributed-tracing/external-calls/) breakdowns.
- **Distributed tracing**: Follow a single request across all microservices with a [flamegraph view](/docs/apm-and-distributed-tracing/trace-details/) that shows every span, its duration, and parent-child relationships.
- **Service map**: [Visualize dependencies](/docs/userguide/service-map/) between services and spot latency or error hot-spots across your architecture.
- **Trace funnels**: [Analyze how requests flow](/docs/trace-funnels/overview/) through a sequence of services or spans and identify drop-off points.
