Cost Meter in SigNoz helps you monitor and control your observability spend. Get real-time visibility into data ingestion across logs, traces, and metrics so you can understand cost drivers, spot unexpected spikes, and take action before bills grow.

## Key capabilities

- **[Real-time cost dashboard](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/cost-meter-dashboard/)**: Track ingestion volumes and costs for logs, traces, and metrics with hourly granularity — broken down by record count, span count, datapoint count, and size.
- **[Meter Explorer](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/meter-explorer/)**: Query your usage data with filters and groupings to identify which services, teams, or attributes drive the most ingestion.
- **[Cost alerts](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/alerts/)**: Set threshold alerts on ingestion volumes or costs and get notified before you exceed your budget.

- [Cost Meter Dashboard](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/cost-meter-dashboard/): Track ingestion volumes across logs, traces, and metrics, compare costs by signal, and spot usage spikes.

## Reduce your ingestion volume

Cost Meter shows what you spend. The Cost Control guides show how to reduce it, in three phases: analysis, execution, and validation.

- [Cost Control](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-control/overview/): The full optimization flow, from analysis to validation.
- [Analyze Ingestion Volume](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-control/understand-ingestion-volume/): Find which signal, service, and environment drives your volume.
- [Optimize Metrics](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-control/optimize-metrics/): Drop unused metrics and aggregate high-cardinality labels.
- [Optimize Traces](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-control/optimize-traces/): Reduce trace volume with head-based or tail-based sampling.
- [Optimize Logs](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-control/optimize-logs/): Filter verbose log records at the Collector.
- [Attribute Ingestion Volume](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-control/attribute-ingestion-volume/): Split volume across teams, services, and environments.
- [Ingestion Limits](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-control/ingestion-limits/): Set hard per-signal caps on an ingestion key.
