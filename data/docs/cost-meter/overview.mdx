Cost Meter is SigNoz's built-in solution for monitoring and controlling observability costs across all telemetry signals. It gives you detailed visibility into data ingestion patterns so you can understand cost drivers, optimize spend, and make informed decisions about your monitoring strategy.

## What you can do with Cost Meter

- Monitor costs broken down by signal (logs, traces, metrics)
- Identify the top contributors to your observability spend
- Query granular meter metrics by service, environment, host, or ingestion key
- Set up alerts to catch unexpected spikes before they impact your budget

## Pricing model

SigNoz uses a usage-based pricing model — you are charged based on the telemetry data ingested. For pricing details, see the [Pricing Page](https://signoz.io/pricing/).

## How it works

Meter data is aggregated over **hourly intervals**. Queries with time ranges under 1 hour return no data; use 1-hour or greater ranges for all analysis.
