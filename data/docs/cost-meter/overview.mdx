## What is Cost Meter in SigNoz

Cost Meter in SigNoz helps you monitor and control your observability spend. Get real-time visibility into data ingestion across logs, traces, and metrics so you can understand cost drivers, spot unexpected spikes, and take action before bills grow.

## Key capabilities

- **Real-time cost dashboard**: Track ingestion volumes and associated costs for all three telemetry signals with hourly granularity — no lag in cost visibility.
- **Meter Explorer**: Query your usage data with filters and groupings to identify which services, teams, or attributes drive the most ingestion.
- **Cost alerts**: Set threshold alerts on ingestion volumes or costs and get notified before you exceed your budget.
- **Signal-level breakdown**: See separate cost summaries for logs (record count and size), traces (span count and size), and metrics (datapoint count) in a single view.

## Key product capabilities

### Working with Cost Meter

Use the main Cost Meter dashboard to understand total ingestion and compare how logs, traces, and metrics contribute to your overall observability spend.

Read more in the [Working with Cost Meter overview](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/working-with-cost-meter/overview/).

### Meter Explorer

Use Meter Explorer to go beyond the summary dashboard and query raw usage data with filters, groupings, and custom time windows.

Read more in the [Meter Explorer overview](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/meter-explorer/) and the [Meter Examples](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/meter-examples/).

### Alerts

Set up meter alerts to monitor ingestion volumes or derived costs for specific signals, services, environments, or ingestion keys before they exceed your expected limits.

Read more in the [Alerts overview](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/alerts/), the [Alert Setup Guide](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/alert-setup-guides/), and the [Alerts Examples](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/alerts-examples/).
