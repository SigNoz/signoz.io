Noz is SigNoz's AI teammate, an AI assistant built into the SigNoz UI. Ask questions about your traces, logs, metrics, dashboards, alerts, and infrastructure in plain English, and Noz investigates across your telemetry and replies with answers grounded in your real data. It works through a problem step by step, points to the likely root cause, and suggests a next action, without you writing queries or leaving SigNoz.


## Open Noz

Click **Noz** in the top-right of the SigNoz header from any page. Noz opens as a side panel next to your current view, so you can keep your dashboard, trace, or logs list in context while you ask.

<Figure src="/img/docs/noz/noz-side-panel.webp" alt="Noz AI assistant open as a side panel next to the SigNoz Home page" caption="Open Noz from the top-right of any SigNoz page; it opens as a side panel beside your current view." />

The panel header gives you a few controls:

- **Recent conversations**: jump back into an earlier conversation.
- **New conversation**: start a fresh thread.
- **Expand**: open Noz full screen at the `/ai-assistant` route, with a searchable conversation list on the left and more room to read long answers.
- **Close**: dismiss the panel.

## Ask your first question

In a new conversation, Noz suggests starter prompts to get you going:

- *Show me the top errors in the last hour*
- *What services have the highest latency?*
- *Give me an overview of system health*
- *Find slow database queries*
- *Which endpoints have the most 5xx errors?*

<Figure src="/img/docs/noz/noz-starter-prompts.webp" alt="Noz new-conversation screen with starter prompts for top errors, highest latency, system health, slow database queries, and 5xx endpoints" caption="A new Noz conversation suggests starter prompts to get you going." />

Type your own question in the **Ask anything** box (use `Shift+Enter` for a new line) or use voice input, then send it. Noz investigates and responds.

## Add context

Use **Add Context** below the input to point Noz at a specific entity before you ask. You can attach:

- **Dashboards**: ask Noz to explain or diagnose a specific dashboard.
- **Alerts**: ask why an alert is firing, or whether it is noisy.
- **Services**: focus the investigation on a single service.

Adding context narrows Noz's scope so its answer covers only the dashboard, alert, or service you care about.

## How Noz investigates

Noz works as an agent: it plans and runs multiple steps against your data, then reasons over the results. Expand **Worked through N steps** on any answer to see the trace. For example: *think → search available tools → run builder queries across traces, logs, and metrics → pull representative traces or logs → reason to a conclusion*.

Because every step runs against your live telemetry, Noz tailors each answer to your environment.

### Example: investigating what changed after a deploy

> **You:** Errors on the checkout service jumped after the 2pm deploy. What changed?

Noz compares checkout's error rate before and after 2pm, pulls the error signatures that are new since the release, and inspects a failing trace, then reports back something like:

> Checkout's 5xx rate went from ~0.2% to 6% right after the 2pm deploy. The failures are new since the release:
>
> - **New 500s on `POST /checkout/confirm`.** They start at 14:03, minutes after the deploy, and were absent in the prior hour.
> - **A downstream call is the trigger.** Failing traces show `payments-api` timing out at 3s on a call this release added.
> - **Latency moved with it.** p99 on the endpoint rose from 420ms to 3.1s, in line with the new timeout.

It then points to the likely cause and offers **Suggested Actions** to keep investigating.

<Figure src="/img/docs/noz/noz-agentic-answer.webp" alt="Noz answer showing the Worked through N steps trace, a post-deploy error-spike diagnosis for the checkout service, and Suggested Actions buttons" caption="Noz works through multiple steps, explains what the deploy changed, and ends with Suggested Actions." />

## Suggested actions and follow-ups

After most answers, Noz shows **Suggested Actions**, one-click follow-ups tailored to the result (for example, *Pull failing traces*, *Compare to the previous deploy*, or *Adjust the alert threshold*). When a suggested fix would change your configuration, Noz asks for confirmation first ("Want me to do that?"), so you stay in control of any change.

## Manage conversations

Conversations are saved so you can return to an investigation later:

- They are grouped by recency (**Today**, **Last 30 days**) with an **Archived** section.
- **Search** past conversations from the full-screen view.
- Start a **new conversation** or **archive** ones you are done with.

Each answer also has **copy**, **thumbs up / thumbs down**, and **regenerate** controls. Use the feedback buttons to tell us when an answer was helpful or off, which improves Noz over time.

## Noz vs. the SigNoz MCP Server

Noz and the [SigNoz MCP Server](https://signoz.io/docs/ai/signoz-mcp-server/) are two ways to bring AI to your observability data:

- **Noz** is the in-product AI teammate inside the SigNoz UI. Reach for it when you are already in SigNoz and want to explore data, understand a dashboard, or triage an incident without writing queries.
- **The SigNoz MCP Server** connects external coding agents (Claude Code, Cursor, and others) to SigNoz, so you can query telemetry from your IDE or terminal alongside your code.

Both are grounded in the same unified telemetry. Use whichever fits where you are working.
