This guide helps you plan an incremental migration from New Relic to SigNoz Cloud. Migrate one telemetry signal at a time, keep New Relic available during validation, and recreate the dashboards and alerts that your team needs.

<Admonition type="info" title="Using Self-Hosted SigNoz?">
  Verify each signal guide against your deployed version, then use the endpoint and authentication that your team operates. Review the [Cloud and Self-Hosted ingestion differences](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/cloud-vs-self-hosted/).
</Admonition>

## Why Migrate to SigNoz?

While New Relic is a powerful platform, migrating to SigNoz offers several distinct advantages:

- **Open Source & Open Standards**: SigNoz is open-source and built natively on OpenTelemetry, ensuring you are not locked into proprietary agents or data formats.
- **Usage-based pricing**: SigNoz Cloud pricing is based on telemetry ingestion. Read the pricing comparison: [SigNoz vs Datadog vs New Relic vs Grafana](https://signoz.io/blog/pricing-comparison-signoz-vs-datadog-vs-newrelic-vs-grafana/)
- **Deployment Flexibility**: Choose SigNoz Cloud for a managed experience or Self-Hosted SigNoz when your team will operate the platform and infrastructure.
- See a detailed comparison: [SigNoz vs New Relic](https://signoz.io/newrelic-alternative/)

## Prerequisites

- A SigNoz Cloud workspace, ingestion endpoint, and ingestion key.
- Access to your New Relic agents and account configuration.
- Permission to update instrumentation and collector configuration.
- A validation window in which both backends can receive test data.

## Migration Guides

Follow these specific guides to migrate each aspect of your observability stack:

- **[Migrate Metrics](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-newrelic/metrics/)**: Transition your application and infrastructure metrics to OpenTelemetry and SigNoz.

- **[Migrate Traces/APM](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-newrelic/traces/)**: Replace New Relic APM agents with OpenTelemetry instrumentation for distributed tracing/APM.

- **[Migrate Logs](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-newrelic/logs/)**: Configure log collection using OpenTelemetry Collector or FluentBit to send logs to SigNoz.

- **[Migrate Dashboards](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-newrelic/dashboards/)**: Recreate your New Relic dashboards in SigNoz using our powerful query builder and visualization options.

- **[Migrate Alerts](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-newrelic/alerts/)**: Set up alerts in SigNoz to match your existing New Relic alert policies.
