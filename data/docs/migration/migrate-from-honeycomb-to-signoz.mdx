This guide helps you plan an incremental migration from Honeycomb to SigNoz Cloud. Existing OpenTelemetry instrumentation can remain in place while you change its export destination, validate incoming data, and recreate the dashboards and alerts that your team needs.

<Admonition type="info" title="Using Self-Hosted SigNoz?">
  Verify each signal guide against your deployed version, then use the endpoint and authentication that your team operates. Review the [Cloud and Self-Hosted ingestion differences](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/cloud-vs-self-hosted/).
</Admonition>

## Prerequisites

- A SigNoz Cloud workspace, ingestion endpoint, and ingestion key.
- Access to your Honeycomb SDK, OpenTelemetry, dashboard, and alert configurations.
- Permission to update instrumentation and collector configuration.
- A validation window in which both backends can receive test data.

## Why Migrate to SigNoz?

- **Unified Observability**: Logs, metrics, and traces in one platform.
- **Usage-based pricing**: SigNoz Cloud pricing is based on telemetry ingestion.
- **Self-host option**: Self-Hosted SigNoz lets your team operate the platform in its own environment.
- **OpenTelemetry Native**: Built on OpenTelemetry.

## Migration Strategy

### For OpenTelemetry Users

If you're already using OpenTelemetry with Honeycomb, [**change your OTLP endpoint**](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-honeycomb/data/) to point to SigNoz.

### For Legacy SDK Users

1. **Migrate to OpenTelemetry**: Follow Honeycomb's guides to move off legacy SDKs:
   - <a href="https://docs.honeycomb.io/troubleshoot/product-lifecycle/recommended-migrations/migrate-from-beelines/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Migrate from Beelines</a>
   - <a href="https://docs.honeycomb.io/troubleshoot/product-lifecycle/recommended-migrations/migrate-from-honeycomb-distributions/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Migrate from Honeycomb Distributions</a>
2. **Point to SigNoz**: Update your [OTLP endpoint](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-honeycomb/data/).
