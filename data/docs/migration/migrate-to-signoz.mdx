Use this overview to plan an incremental migration from Datadog, New Relic, Grafana, ELK, Honeycomb, or another backend to SigNoz Cloud. Keep your current backend available while you validate each telemetry signal and rebuild the dashboards or alerts you need.

<Admonition type="info" title="Using Self-Hosted SigNoz?">
  Verify each signal guide against your deployed version, then replace the Cloud endpoint and ingestion key with the endpoint and authentication that your team operates. Review the [Cloud and Self-Hosted ingestion differences](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/cloud-vs-self-hosted/).
</Admonition>


## Why Migrate to SigNoz?

- **Unified observability:** View all your telemetry data in one UI for end-to-end debugging without tool hopping.
- **OpenTelemetry native:** Built on the open standard from day one, so you can keep portable instrumentation.
- **Flexible deployment options:** [SigNoz Cloud](https://signoz.io/docs/cloud/) is fully managed. [SigNoz Enterprise](https://signoz.io/enterprise/) also offers BYOC managed by SigNoz and Self-Hosted SigNoz with support. Use the [Self-Hosted SigNoz installation guide](https://signoz.io/docs/install/self-host/) for a user-managed setup.
- **Simple, transparent usage-based pricing:** Pay only for the [data you ingest](https://signoz.io/pricing/). No surprise host, agent, or feature add-on fees.
