This guide helps you plan an incremental migration from Loki, Grafana, Tempo, and Mimir to SigNoz Cloud. Migrate one telemetry signal at a time, keep the LGTM stack available during validation, and recreate the dashboards and alerts that your team needs.

<Admonition type="info" title="Using Self-Hosted SigNoz?">
  Verify each signal guide against your deployed version, then use the endpoint and authentication that your team operates. Review the [Cloud and Self-Hosted ingestion differences](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/cloud-vs-self-hosted/).
</Admonition>

## Prerequisites

- A SigNoz Cloud workspace, ingestion endpoint, and ingestion key.
- Access to your Loki, Grafana, Tempo, Mimir, and OpenTelemetry configurations.
- Permission to update instrumentation and collector configuration.
- A validation window in which both backends can receive test data.

## Why Migrate to SigNoz?

While the LGTM stack is powerful, migrating to SigNoz offers several advantages:

- **Unified Platform**: Instead of managing multiple components, SigNoz provides a single, cohesive platform for all your observability needs.
- **Native OpenTelemetry Support**: Built from the ground up to support OpenTelemetry, ensuring future-proof observability.
- **Managed operations**: SigNoz Cloud removes platform installation, storage, scaling, and upgrade work from your team.
- **Consistent User Experience**: Unified UI for all observability signals without context switching.

## Migration Guides

Please refer to the following guides for step-by-step instructions on migrating each component from LGTM Stack to SigNoz:

- [**Migrating Metrics**](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-grafana/metrics/): Transfer your Mimir/Prometheus metrics to SigNoz.

- [**Migrating Traces/APM**](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-grafana/traces/): Move your distributed traces/APM from Tempo to SigNoz.

- [**Migrating Logs**](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-grafana/logs/): Transition your Loki logs to SigNoz.

- [**Migrating Dashboards**](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-grafana/dashboards/): Convert your Grafana dashboards to SigNoz dashboards.

- [**Migrating Alerts**](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-grafana/alerts/): Transfer your alerting rules and configurations.
