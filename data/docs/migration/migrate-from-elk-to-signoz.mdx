This guide helps you plan an incremental migration from Elasticsearch, Logstash, and Kibana to SigNoz Cloud. Migrate one telemetry signal at a time, keep ELK available during validation, and recreate the dashboards and alerts that your team needs.

<Admonition type="info" title="Using Self-Hosted SigNoz?">
  Verify each signal guide against your deployed version, then use the endpoint and authentication that your team operates. Review the [Cloud and Self-Hosted ingestion differences](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/cloud-vs-self-hosted/).
</Admonition>

## Why Migrate to SigNoz?

While the ELK Stack is a powerful platform, migrating to SigNoz offers several distinct advantages:

- **Unified Platform**: SigNoz provides a single, cohesive platform that eliminates the complexity of managing multiple components such as Elasticsearch clusters, Logstash pipelines, and Kibana.
- **Native OpenTelemetry Support**: Built from the ground up to support OpenTelemetry, ensuring seamless integration with modern instrumentation standards and future-proof observability.
- **Integrated Signals**: SigNoz correlates logs, metrics, and traces out-of-the-box, providing a more seamless debugging experience compared to correlating data across Kibana and separate APM UIs.
- **Managed operations**: SigNoz Cloud manages platform storage, scaling, upgrades, security, and availability.
- **Usage-based pricing**: SigNoz Cloud pricing is based on telemetry ingestion rather than host or container count.

## Prerequisites

- A SigNoz Cloud workspace, ingestion endpoint, and ingestion key.
- Access to your Filebeat, Logstash, Kibana, and Elastic APM configurations.
- Permission to update instrumentation and collector configuration.
- A validation window in which both backends can receive test data.

## Migration Guides

Follow these specific guides to migrate each aspect of your observability stack:

- **[Migrate Metrics](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-elk/metrics/)**: Transition your Metricbeat modules and infrastructure metrics to OpenTelemetry and SigNoz.

- **[Migrate Traces/APM](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-elk/traces/)**: Replace Elastic APM agents with OpenTelemetry instrumentation for distributed tracing/APM.

- **[Migrate Logs](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-elk/logs/)**: Configure log collection using OpenTelemetry Collector or FluentBit to send logs to SigNoz.

- **[Migrate Dashboards](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-elk/dashboards/)**: Recreate your Kibana dashboards in SigNoz using our powerful query builder and visualization options.

- **[Migrate Alerts](https://signoz.io/docs/migration/migrate-from-elk/alerts/)**: Set up alerts in SigNoz to match your existing Kibana or ElastAlert rules.
