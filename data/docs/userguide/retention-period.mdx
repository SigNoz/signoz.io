By default, retention period is set to 15 days for logs and traces, and 30 days for metrics.

<Admonition>
When retention settings are updated, the changes apply only to newly ingested data. Existing data retains the previous retention settings, as updating retention for existing data is a resource-intensive process.

Once data reaches its TTL, it is **permanently deleted** and cannot be recovered. Increasing the retention period later does not restore previously expired data.
</Admonition>

<Tabs entityName="plans">
<TabItem value="signoz-cloud" label="SigNoz Cloud" default>

Retention period changes in SigNoz Cloud aren't available in the UI. To request a change, use the chatbox in the bottom-right of your SigNoz Cloud interface or email [cloud-support@signoz.io](mailto:cloud-support@signoz.io).

Available retention periods:

- Logs: 15, 30, 90, 180 days, or 1 year
- Metrics: 1, 3, 6, or 13 months
- Traces: 15, 30, 90, 180 days, or 1 year

Retention changes may affect pricing. Use the [pricing calculator](https://signoz.io/pricing/#estimate-your-monthly-bill) to estimate updated costs. For the full request process, see the [retention FAQ](https://signoz.io/docs/faqs/general/).

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="self-host" label="Self-Host">

To set retention period for metrics, traces, and logs, navigate to the `General` tab on the `Settings` page.

<figure data-zoomable align='center'>
    <img src="/img/docs/retention_settings.webp" alt="Set Retention period of metrics traces"/>
</figure>

- You can select independent retention periods for metrics, traces, and logs.
- Click `Save` to update the new retention periods.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
