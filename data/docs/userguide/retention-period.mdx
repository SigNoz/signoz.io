## Default Retention Periods

By default, SigNoz retains logs and traces for 15 days, and metrics for 30 days.

<Admonition>
When retention settings are updated, the changes apply only to newly ingested data. Existing data retains the previous retention settings, as updating retention for existing data is a resource-intensive process.

Once data reaches its TTL, it is **permanently deleted** and cannot be recovered. Increasing the retention period later does not restore previously expired data.
</Admonition>

## Change Your Retention Period

<Tabs entityName="plans">
<TabItem value="signoz-cloud" label="SigNoz Cloud" default>

The `Workspace` tab on the `Settings` page shows your current retention periods.

Pick your new retention period for each signal:

- Logs: 15, 30, 90, 180 days, or 1 year
- Metrics: 1, 3, 6, or 13 months
- Traces: 15, 30, 90, 180 days, or 1 year

Retention changes affect pricing. Use the [pricing calculator](https://signoz.io/pricing/#estimate-your-monthly-bill) to estimate your new bill.

Then contact support with the periods you want, either through the chatbox in the bottom-right of your SigNoz Cloud interface or by email at [cloud-support@signoz.io](mailto:cloud-support@signoz.io).

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="self-host" label="Self-Host">

1. Go to the `Workspace` tab on the `Settings` page.
2. Set independent retention periods for metrics, traces, and logs under `Retention Controls`.
3. Click `Save` on each signal you changed.
