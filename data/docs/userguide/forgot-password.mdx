## Overview

If you have forgotten your SigNoz Cloud password, you can reset it by requesting a password reset link from the login page. The link is sent to your registered email address and expires after **6 hours**.

<Admonition type="info">
The forgot password flow is available for SigNoz Cloud accounts that use **email and password** to sign in. If your organization uses SSO (Google, SAML, OIDC), contact your identity provider to manage your credentials.
</Admonition>

## Prerequisites

- A SigNoz Cloud account with email and password authentication.
- Access to the inbox of the email address used to register.

## Steps

### Step 1: Click "Forgot password?" on the login page

The SigNoz login flow is two steps:

1. Enter your email address and click **Next**.

<figure data-zoomable align='center'>
    <img src="/img/docs/forgot-password/forgot-password-login-step1.webp" alt="SigNoz login page showing email address input and Next button"/>
    <figcaption><i>Enter your email address and click Next.</i></figcaption>
</figure>

2. If your account uses email and password authentication, a second screen appears with the password field and a **Forgot password?** link. Click **Forgot password?** to begin the reset flow.

<Admonition type="info">
If your account uses SSO (Google, SAML, OIDC), the second screen will redirect you to your identity provider instead of showing the password field. In that case, the forgot password flow does not apply — manage your credentials through your SSO provider.
</Admonition>

<figure data-zoomable align='center'>
    <img src="/img/docs/forgot-password/forgot-password-login-step2.webp" alt="SigNoz login page second step showing the password field and Forgot password? link"/>
    <figcaption><i>The password screen appears after entering your email. Click "Forgot password?" to start the reset flow.</i></figcaption>
</figure>

### Step 2: Enter your email and send the reset link

On the **Forgot your password?** page, enter the email address linked to your SigNoz Cloud account and click **Send reset link**.

<figure data-zoomable align='center'>
    <img src="/img/docs/forgot-password/forgot-password-form.webp" alt="Forgot your password page showing email address input and Send reset link button"/>
    <figcaption><i>Enter your email and click "Send reset link" to receive the reset email.</i></figcaption>
</figure>

SigNoz sends a password reset email to that address. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.

### Step 3: Check your email

After submitting, the page confirms that a reset link has been sent to your inbox.

<figure data-zoomable align='center'>
    <img src="/img/docs/forgot-password/forgot-password-check-email.webp" alt="Check your email confirmation page after requesting a password reset in SigNoz"/>
    <figcaption><i>SigNoz confirms the reset link has been sent. Check your inbox.</i></figcaption>
</figure>

### Step 4: Open the reset email and click Reset Password

Open the email from **signoz-support@signoz.cloud** with the subject **A Password Reset Was Requested for SigNoz** and click the **Reset Password** button.

<Admonition type="warning">
The reset link expires in **6 hours**. If the link has expired, return to the login page and request a new one.
</Admonition>

### Step 5: Set your new password

Clicking the **Reset Password** button opens the password reset page in your browser. Enter and confirm your new password, then save.

You are now signed in with your new credentials.

## Troubleshooting

**I did not receive the reset email.**
- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Confirm you entered the correct email address — the one used to register with SigNoz Cloud.
- Wait a few minutes and try again. If the problem persists, contact your admin or reach out to <a href="mailto:cloud-support@signoz.io">cloud-support@signoz.io</a>.

**The reset link shows an error or says it has expired.**
- Reset links expire after 6 hours. Return to the login page, click **Forgot password?**, and request a new link.

**I entered my email but was redirected to my SSO provider instead of seeing a password field.**
- Your account uses SSO (Google, SAML, OIDC). The forgot password flow only applies to email and password accounts. Manage your credentials through your identity provider or contact your IT administrator.
