Vercel supports automatic log and OpenTelemetry (OTel) trace export to SigNoz without code changes using <a href="https://vercel.com/docs/drains" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">log and OpenTelemetry drains</a>.

<KeyPointCallout defaultCollapsed={true}>
Most steps are identical. To adapt this guide, update the endpoint and remove the ingestion key header as shown in [Cloud → Self-Hosted](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/cloud-vs-self-hosted/#cloud-to-self-hosted).
</KeyPointCallout>

## Prerequisites

- A <a href="https://vercel.com/docs/plans" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Vercel Pro or Enterprise</a> account
- An instance of [SigNoz Cloud](https://signoz.io/teams/)

## Configure Vercel Drains

Follow the steps below to send your trace or log data from Vercel to SigNoz Cloud:

### Step 1: Create a New Drain

1. Navigate to **Team Settings** > **Drains** and click **Add Drain**. This opens a dialog to configure your new drain.
2. Select the type of observability data you want to drain. Choose **Logs** or **Traces** based on your needs. The configuration options for your selected drain type will appear.
3. Provide a name for your drain, select which projects should send data to your endpoint, and configure the sampling rate (and log source/environment for logs).
4. After configuring these settings, click the **Next** button to proceed.

### Step 2: Configure Destination

Choose how you want to receive your drain data. Under the **Custom Endpoint** tab, configure the delivery format and endpoint.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem value="logs" label="Logs" default>
    1. Set the delivery format to `JSON`. Vercel will now be configured to send JSON payloads to your endpoint.
    2. Enter the SigNoz **logs** ingestion **endpoint**:

    ```
    https://ingest.<region>.signoz.cloud:443/logs/json
    ```
    Replace `<region>` with your [SigNoz Cloud region](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/signoz-cloud/overview/#endpoint).
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem value="traces" label="Traces">
    1. Set the delivery format to `JSON` or `Protobuf` (Protobuf is recommended for performance).
    2. Enter the SigNoz **OTLP HTTP traces** ingestion **endpoint**:

    ```
    https://ingest.<region>.signoz.cloud:443/v1/traces
    ```

    Replace `<region>` with your [SigNoz Cloud region](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/signoz-cloud/overview/#endpoint).
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

3. Enable **Custom Headers** and add the following header:

```
signoz-ingestion-key: <your-ingestion-key>
```

Replace `<your-ingestion-key>` with the [ingestion key](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/signoz-cloud/keys/) from your SigNoz Cloud account. You can find this under **Settings > Ingestion Settings** in SigNoz. This ensures SigNoz Cloud authenticates and accepts your Vercel telemetry.

### Step 3: Test and Create the Drain

Click on **Test** to ensure your endpoint receives the data correctly. If successful, you will start to see sample telemetry on the SigNoz UI. After verifying the test results, click **Create Drain** in your Vercel dashboard.

Your Vercel telemetry will now be streamed to SigNoz Cloud.

## Validate

After creating the drain, verify that the data is flowing into SigNoz:

<Tabs>
  <TabItem value="logs" label="Logs" default>
    1. Open **Logs Explorer** in your SigNoz Cloud dashboard.
    2. Wait a few minutes for logs to arrive — there may be a short delay.
    3. Look for log entries with Vercel-specific attributes such as `source`, `projectName`, and `deploymentId`.
    4. If you see log entries from your Vercel projects, the integration is working correctly.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem value="traces" label="Traces">
    1. Open **Traces** in your SigNoz Cloud dashboard.
    2. Wait a few minutes for traces to arrive — there may be a short delay.
    3. Look for trace entries originating from your Vercel projects.
    4. If you see trace entries from your Vercel applications, the integration is working correctly.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Troubleshooting
</ToggleHeading>

### No data appearing in SigNoz

- **Incorrect region**: Verify that `<region>` in the endpoint URL matches your [SigNoz Cloud region](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/signoz-cloud/overview/#endpoint).
- **Invalid ingestion key**: Confirm the `signoz-ingestion-key` header value matches the key shown in your [SigNoz Cloud settings](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/signoz-cloud/keys/).
- **Drain not active**: Check the drain status in **Team Settings > Drains** in Vercel. If it shows an error, click the drain to see details.
- **Verification**: After applying fixes, click **Test** in your Vercel drain config and check the SigNoz Logs Explorer or Traces again.

### Drain creation or test fails

- **Endpoint URL format**: Ensure the URL matches exactly based on your drain type (`/logs/json` or `/v1/traces`) with no trailing slash or extra path segments.
- **Missing headers**: Confirm the `signoz-ingestion-key` custom header is set and the value does not contain leading/trailing whitespace.
- **Verification**: Once corrected, the **Test** button in Vercel should return a successful response.
