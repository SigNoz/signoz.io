Common questions about SigNoz Cloud billing. For exact rates and plan details, see the [pricing page](https://signoz.io/pricing/).

### Q. How does SigNoz pricing work?

SigNoz Cloud is usage-based. SigNoz bills you for the telemetry you ingest: logs and traces by volume (per GB), and metrics by sample count (per million samples). There are no per-seat, per-host, or custom-metric charges. See [How you're charged](https://signoz.io/docs/billing/how-you-are-charged/).

### Q. Do I pay per user or per host?

No. Teammates and monitored hosts are unlimited on the Teams plan and don't affect your bill. You only pay for ingested telemetry.

### Q. What counts as "ingestion"?

For logs and traces, it's the volume of data ingested, measured in GB. For metrics, it's the number of samples, where a sample is each data point for each unique time series at each scrape. More unique series (cardinality) and more frequent scrapes both increase sample count.

### Q. Does retention affect my cost?

Yes. Longer retention costs more because your data is stored for longer, so a shorter retention period lowers your bill. Each signal has its own retention (logs/traces: 15, 30, 90, 180 days, or 1 year, minimum 15 days; metrics: 1, 3, 6, or 13 months, minimum 1 month). Because of those minimums, reducing retention only saves money down to 15 days (logs/traces) or 1 month (metrics), not below. See [retention](https://signoz.io/docs/billing/how-you-are-charged/#retention).

### Q. How do I change my retention period?

Contact the SigNoz team through in-product chat or email with the retention you want for each signal. Changes apply only to newly ingested data; existing data keeps its earlier retention. Once data passes its retention period, SigNoz deletes it permanently, and increasing retention later doesn't restore it.

### Q. What happens if I exceed my included usage?

On the Teams plan, SigNoz charges usage beyond the included amount at standard per-signal rates as overage. Your bill scales predictably with ingestion, and you never pay for capacity you don't use. To catch overages before they grow, set [cost alerts](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/alerts/) or [limits on your ingestion keys](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/signoz-cloud/keys/#add-limits-to-a-key).

### Q. What happens when my trial ends?

If you don't add a payment method before your SigNoz Cloud trial ends, SigNoz deletes your deployment after a 7-day grace period, with email reminders before deletion. To keep your deployment, add a credit card in the Billing section of your account before the trial expires or during the grace period. See the [general FAQs](https://signoz.io/docs/faqs/general/) for details on trial accounts.

### Q. Where do I add a payment method?

In the **Billing** section of your SigNoz Cloud account, where you can also see your current usage.

### Q. How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to **Settings → Billing**, select **Cancel subscription**, and confirm. You can also reach out through the in-product chatbox. See the [general FAQs](https://signoz.io/docs/faqs/general/) for the full flow.

### Q. How can I monitor my spend before the invoice?

Use [Cost Meter](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/overview/) for real-time, hourly visibility into ingestion and cost by signal. Set [cost alerts](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/alerts/) to be notified before you cross a budget threshold.

### Q. How do I reduce my bill?

Send less low-value data and use shorter retention. In practice that means [dropping noisy logs](https://signoz.io/docs/logs-management/guides/drop-logs/), [dropping spans](https://signoz.io/docs/traces-management/guides/drop-spans/), [sampling traces](https://signoz.io/docs/traces-management/guides/tail-sampling/), and [controlling metric cardinality](https://signoz.io/docs/metrics-management/reducing-costs/). Use [Cost Meter](https://signoz.io/docs/cost-meter/overview/) to find your biggest cost drivers first.

### Q. Is the Community Edition free?

Yes. The open-source, self-hosted Community Edition is free to use, with no ingestion bill. You provision and manage the infrastructure yourself. See the [Billing overview](https://signoz.io/docs/billing/overview/).

### Q. Is there a discount for startups?

Eligible early-stage companies can get a discount on the Teams plan through the SigNoz startup program. See the <a href="https://signoz.io/startups/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">SigNoz for Startups</a> page for current eligibility and details.

### Q. How is Enterprise different?

Enterprise adds volume discounts, annual contracts, guided migration, and a choice of deployment: a dedicated environment on SigNoz Cloud, bring your own cloud, or self-hosted with a support contract. <a href="https://signoz.io/enterprise/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Contact the SigNoz team</a> to discuss.

### Q. Can I pay through AWS Marketplace?

Yes. You can subscribe to SigNoz Cloud through AWS Marketplace and have it billed through your AWS account. After subscribing on AWS Marketplace, connect your SigNoz Cloud account on the [AWS Marketplace activation page](https://signoz.io/aws/signup/) by entering your license key (found under Settings → Account Settings → License). See [Paying through AWS Marketplace](https://signoz.io/docs/billing/how-you-are-charged/#paying-through-aws-marketplace) for the full steps.

### Q. Where can I see SigNoz's security certifications?

SigNoz is SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant. Find certifications and other security details on the <a href="https://trust.signoz.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">SigNoz Trust Center</a>.

### Q. What terms govern my use of SigNoz Cloud?

The <a href="https://signoz.io/terms-of-service/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">SigNoz Terms of Service</a> govern your use of SigNoz Cloud, including billing and payment.
