SigNoz Cloud uses simple, usage-based billing: you pay for the telemetry you ingest, with no per-seat, per-host, or custom-metric surcharges. SigNoz is available in three flavors, and this page explains what each includes so you can pick the right fit. For current pricing, always check the [pricing page](https://signoz.io/pricing/).

## Plans at a glance

| | Teams (Cloud) | Enterprise | Community |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hosting** | Fully managed by SigNoz | Managed or dedicated | Self-hosted (you run it) |
| **Pricing** | Usage-based, from $49/mo | Custom, from $4,000/mo | Free, open source |
| **Best for** | Fast-scaling teams that want observability without ops overhead | Larger orgs needing dedicated environments or volume discounts | Teams with DevOps capacity who want full control |
| **Infrastructure** | Handled for you | Handled for you | You provision and scale |

## Teams (Cloud)

The fully managed cloud offering. SigNoz runs, scales, and maintains everything; you send telemetry and pay based on what you ingest. See [How you're charged](https://signoz.io/docs/billing/how-you-are-charged/) for the mechanics. You can also pay through [AWS Marketplace](https://signoz.io/docs/billing/how-you-are-charged/#paying-through-aws-marketplace) to bill SigNoz through your AWS account.

The Teams plan includes:

- **A base amount of included usage each month.** You only pay for usage beyond it (overages at standard rates).
- **Unlimited teammates**, with no per-seat pricing.
- **Any number of hosts**, with no per-host pricing.
- **All platform features**, including APM, logs, traces, metrics, dashboards, alerts, and more.

### Startup program

Eligible early-stage companies can receive a discount on the Teams plan through the SigNoz startup program. See the <a href="https://signoz.io/startups/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">SigNoz for Startups</a> page for eligibility and details.

## Enterprise

For larger organizations with additional requirements. On top of everything in the Teams plan, Enterprise adds:

- **Volume discounts** and annual contracts.
- **Dedicated environments** and deployment options.
- **Guided migration assistance.**

To discuss Enterprise, <a href="https://signoz.io/enterprise/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">get in touch with the SigNoz team</a>.

## Community Edition

The open-source, self-hosted edition. It's free to use: you provision the infrastructure, deploy SigNoz, and manage upgrades, scaling, and storage yourself. There is no ingestion bill because you own the underlying compute and storage costs.

Community is a good fit when you have the DevOps capacity to run and scale the stack and want complete control over your data and deployment.

## Choosing between organizations

Billing in SigNoz Cloud is scoped to your account and its usage. If you need isolated billing, environments, or data boundaries for different teams or business units, reach out to the SigNoz team to discuss the best setup.
