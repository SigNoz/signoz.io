## Overview

<a href="https://www.baseten.co" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Baseten</a> is a platform for deploying and serving machine learning models. Baseten ships its logs to SigNoz over OTLP/HTTP using a built-in OpenTelemetry connection.

<Admonition type="warning">
  Baseten exports logs only. It does not send traces or metrics. Each Baseten organization supports one OTLP destination at a time, so point Baseten at SigNoz and remove any other destination first.
</Admonition>

<Admonition type="info" title="Using self-hosted SigNoz?" defaultCollapsed="true">
  Two extra requirements apply because Baseten pushes to your endpoint from outside your network:

  1. **Publicly reachable:** your collector must be accessible from the internet, not on a private network or VPN.
  2. **HTTPS with a valid certificate:** plain HTTP and self-signed certificates are rejected. Put a reverse proxy (e.g. Caddy) in front of port 4318 to terminate TLS.

  Set the **Endpoint URL** to your collector's OTLP/HTTP logs path (for example `https://signoz.example.com/v1/logs`). Baseten requires both header fields, so an empty header blocks **Save** — enter a harmless header your collector ignores (for example **Header name** `x-source`, **Header value** `baseten`). For endpoint and auth differences, see [Cloud to Self-Hosted](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/cloud-vs-self-hosted/#cloud-to-self-hosted).
</Admonition>

## Prerequisites

- A Baseten organization, with permission to edit organization settings
- An instance of SigNoz (either [Cloud](https://signoz.io/teams/) or [Self-Hosted](https://signoz.io/docs/install/self-host/))

## Configure the OTEL Connection

### Add the connection in Baseten

1. In the Baseten console, go to **Settings > General** and find the **OTEL connection** card.
2. Click **Add connection**.
3. Configure the fields:

  - **Endpoint URL**: `https://ingest.<region>.signoz.cloud:443/v1/logs`
  - **Header name**: `signoz-ingestion-key`
  - **Header value**: `<your-ingestion-key>`

4. Save the connection.

Verify these values:

- `<region>`: your SigNoz Cloud [region](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/signoz-cloud/overview/#endpoint)
- `<your-ingestion-key>`: your [SigNoz ingestion key](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/signoz-cloud/keys/)

### Test the connection

Click **Test** in the connection dialog (Baseten also fires this probe when you save). It posts a `Baseten OTLP connection test` log to your endpoint. Open **Logs** in SigNoz and search for the `baseten.telemetry_export_test` scope to confirm the endpoint and key work before any model traffic flows.

Exports start once the connection is enabled. Baseten does not backfill older logs.

## Validate

1. Trigger activity in Baseten. For example, send a prediction to a deployed model, or redeploy one.
2. Open **Logs** in SigNoz. Within a few minutes, your Baseten logs appear as log entries.
3. Filter on `service.name = baseten` to see all Baseten logs. You can also narrow it down by `model_id` or `model_version_id` to scope to one model.

## Troubleshooting
</ToggleHeading>

### No data appearing

- Confirm the **Endpoint URL** ends in `/v1/logs` and matches your SigNoz Cloud [region](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/signoz-cloud/overview/#endpoint).
- Confirm the **Header name** is exactly `signoz-ingestion-key` and the **Header value** is a valid ingestion key. Copy it fresh from [SigNoz settings](https://signoz.io/docs/ingestion/signoz-cloud/keys/).
- Confirm the connection is enabled and that Baseten is generating logs. Exports start when you enable the connection and do not include older logs.
- Search `service.name = baseten`. If the `baseten.telemetry_export_test` probe appears but model logs do not, the pipe works and you only need to generate activity (deploy a model, or send a prediction).

### Replacing an existing destination

A Baseten organization supports one OTLP destination at a time. If logs already go elsewhere, update or remove that connection before you point Baseten at SigNoz.

</details>

## Limitations

- **Logs only:** Baseten exports logs. Traces and metrics are not supported.
- **One destination:** each organization supports one OTLP destination at a time.
- **No backfill:** exports cover logs generated after you enable the connection.
