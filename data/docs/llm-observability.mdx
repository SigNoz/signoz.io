Pick your model provider, framework, or tool, and follow its guide to send data to SigNoz. Each card lists the signals that its guide sends. [AI Observability](https://signoz.io/docs/ai-observability/) reads traces only. For a guide that sends only metrics and logs, build a [custom dashboard](https://signoz.io/docs/userguide/manage-dashboards/) for that data. To learn how LLM telemetry works, see [LLM Observability Core Concepts](https://signoz.io/docs/llm-observability-core-concepts/).
